# Buildings and decorations

Paste one prompt at a time. Save each PNG to `src/art/` with the file name
shown. Fences and dirt paths aren't here: they stay drawn in code because
they join up with their neighbors in many combinations.

## a farmhouse

File: `building-farmhouse.png`

```
Make one building, a farmhouse, for an isometric farming game.

Style: bright, friendly, cartoony farm art in the spirit of 2009 browser farm games, but entirely original (nothing may resemble another game's characters or artwork). Rounded, chunky shapes that read clearly when small. Flat color with one soft highlight and one soft shade per surface. Soft outlines about 2 px wide in a darker shade of the fill, never pure black. Light comes from the upper left. Isometric 2:1 view: a floor tile is a diamond 128 px wide and 64 px tall at this size. Fully transparent background: no ground, no grass, no shadows under things, no text, no grid lines, no border. Crisp edges. Export as PNG with transparency at exactly the size given.

Canvas: exactly 384 x 384 pixels, transparent.

The building's footprint is the diamond in the bottom half of the canvas, spanning its full width: top corner at (192, 192), right corner at (384, 288), bottom corner at (192, 384), left corner at (0, 288). The building stands inside that footprint with a little margin all round, its two front walls rising from the footprint's edges (the left-hand wall faces down-left, the right-hand wall faces down-right), and its roof may use the whole height of the canvas.

Look: a cosy single-storey farmhouse with cream walls, a red gable roof whose ridge runs parallel to the left-hand wall, a brown front door on the right-hand wall and two small blue windows on the left-hand wall.
```
