# Animals

Five sprite sheets, one per animal. Paste one prompt at a time. Save each
PNG to `src/art/` with the file name shown.

## Chicken

File: `animal-chicken.png`

```
Make a sprite sheet of one farm animal, a chicken, for an isometric farming game.

Style: bright, friendly, cartoony farm art in the spirit of 2009 browser farm games, but entirely original (nothing may resemble another game's characters or artwork). Rounded, chunky shapes that read clearly when small. Flat color with one soft highlight and one soft shade per surface. Soft outlines about 2 px wide in a darker shade of the fill, never pure black. Light comes from the upper left. Isometric 2:1 view: a floor tile is a diamond 128 px wide and 64 px tall at this size. Fully transparent background: no ground, no grass, no shadows under things, no text, no grid lines, no border. Crisp edges. Export as PNG with transparency at exactly the size given.

Canvas: exactly 896 x 256 pixels, transparent. It is a grid of 7 columns and 2 rows of 128 x 128 pixel cells. Draw one pose per cell, with no lines between cells.

Row 0 (y 0 to 127): the chicken facing down and to the left, toward the viewer, three-quarter view.
Row 1 (y 128 to 255): the chicken facing up and to the left, seen from behind and the side.

Columns, the same in both rows:
- Column 0: standing still.
- Columns 1 to 4: a walk cycle of four frames that loops smoothly.
- Columns 5 and 6: an idle action of two frames that alternate: the chicken pecks at the ground, head down then head up.

In every cell the lowest point of the feet is at y 112 of that cell, centered at x 64. The chicken is 40 pixels tall from feet to top of head, and the same size in every frame. Keep everything inside its cell.

Look: small, round and white, with a red comb, red wattle and short yellow beak. Draw nothing it produces (no eggs, milk or wool tufts lying around).
```
